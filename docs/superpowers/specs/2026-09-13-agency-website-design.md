# Agency Website Design Specification

**Date:** 2026-09-13  
**Topic:** Digital Agency Website for Local Business Growth  
**Status:** Approved  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Positioning

### 1.1 Brand Identity
* **Agency Name:** **MEDIA-TARDS** (Sub-mark: *Digital Growth Studio*)
* **Tagline:** *Turning local businesses into the obvious choice in their market.*
* **Positioning:** A modern digital growth partner for businesses that want to build a commanding online presence and turn attention into paying customers.
* **The Founders:** 3 collegiate builders combining conversion strategy, UI/UX design, and cutting-edge frontend engineering. Direct founder access, rapid 2–3 week launch cycles, zero agency bloat.

### 1.2 Core Philosophy: DISCOVER → TRUST → CONVERT
1. **DISCOVER:** Helping potential customers find the business through local search, instant load speeds, and cohesive digital presence.
2. **TRUST:** Giving visitors overwhelming visual and psychological reasons to believe through clean design, social proof, and seamless mobile UX.
3. **CONVERT:** Removing all friction to turn attention into scheduled appointments, inquiries, and revenue.

---

## 2. Ideal Client & Pain Architecture
* **Revenues:** $250,000 – $2,000,000 / year.
* **Sectors:** Dental & Cosmetic Clinics, Boutique Fitness / Gyms, Custom Architecture & Craftsmen, Artisan Hospitality.
* **Their Problem:** Their real-world service is 9/10, but their online presence is a 3/10. Outdated WordPress sites, broken mobile menus, unmaintained pages, and lost leads to modern competitors.
* **How We Win Trust:** Radical honesty with live, clickable **Concept Projects** with measurable business goals instead of fake reviews; 100/100 performance; direct founder execution.

## 3. Visual Language & Design System
* **Background Primary:** `#080A0F` (Deep Obsidian Void)
* **Background Secondary / Surface:** `#0F131D` (Dark Zinc Surface)
* **Background Elevated / Card:** `#161B28` (Elevated Card)
* **Border Subdued:** `#1E2638` (Architectural Grid Line)
* **Border Hover / Active:** `#2F3D5B`
* **Text Primary:** `#F8FAFC` (Pure Bone White)
* **Text Secondary:** `#94A3B8` (Cool Muted Gray)
* **Accent Primary:** `#38BDF8` (Luminous Sky Cobalt)
* **Accent Secondary / Warm Glow:** `#F59E0B` (Electric Amber)
* **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans & Inter
* **Radius:** `rounded-xl` (12px), `rounded-full` for badges & pills.

---

## 4. Components & Pages
1. **Navbar:** Sticky glassmorphic bar, mobile drawer, audit quick-CTA.
2. **Hero:** High-conviction proposition, trust strip, dual CTAs.
3. **Problem:** 4 Core Friction Points interactive cards.
4. **Framework:** Interactive 3-stage visual engine (Discover → Trust → Convert).
5. **Case Studies:** 3 realistic local business concept benchmarks (Aura Dental, Forge Fitness, Atelier Studio) with live interactive preview toggle.
6. **Services:** Performance Websites, Conversion Landing Pages, Brand & UX, Local SEO, Smart Booking & Automation.
7. **Process:** 5-Step transparent launch protocol.
8. **Why Us:** Comparison matrix highlighting direct founder execution vs bloated agencies.
9. **Team:** 3 collegiate founders (Strategy, UI/UX, Engineering).
10. **Interactive Audit & Scope Estimator:** Live goal and industry selector with instant estimate & inquiry dispatch.
11. **Footer:** Colophon, availability status, quick links.
